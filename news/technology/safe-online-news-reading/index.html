How to check a news story before you share it

A practical guide to checking the source, date, author and evidence behind an online claim.

By NewsArc Editorial Team · Published September 24, 2026

What to check

Look for the publisher, author, publication date and original source. For important claims, compare more than one credible source.

Why it matters

Old reports can look like new events when they are reposted without context. Reading beyond the headline helps prevent accidental misinformation.
