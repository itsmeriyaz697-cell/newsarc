What students should know before using AI tools

How to use AI as a learning aid while checking facts, protecting privacy and keeping work original.

By NewsArc Editorial Team · Published September 22, 2026

Use it as an aid

AI can help explain a topic or generate practice questions, but students should check answers and sources themselves.

Protect privacy

Do not upload personal information, sensitive school documents or someone else’s private data.
