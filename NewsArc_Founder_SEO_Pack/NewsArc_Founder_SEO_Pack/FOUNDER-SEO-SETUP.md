# NewsArc Founder SEO Pack

This pack creates a public founder/profile identity for Riyaz Chalise — Founder of NewsArc.

## Why this helps
Google supports ProfilePage structured data for pages focused on a person or organization associated with a website. Organization markup can also help Google understand and disambiguate an organization. This does not guarantee rankings, a Knowledge Panel, rich results, traffic, or AdSense approval.

## Files
- src/components/FounderSchema.jsx
- src/components/NewsArcOrganizationSchema.jsx
- src/components/RiyazProfileSchema.jsx
- src/pages/RiyazChalise.jsx
- public/founder-profile-meta.html

## Recommended URL
https://newsarc-ga3f.vercel.app/about/riyaz-chalise

## Important
- Use the profile only if Riyaz Chalise is genuinely the founder of NewsArc.
- On NewsArc articles, use the actual author. Do not mark Riyaz as author of articles he did not write.
- Add real public profile URLs to `sameAs` only when they actually exist.
- If you move to a custom domain, replace the current URLs throughout these files.
- Deploy the profile page, then validate structured data and inspect the URL in Google Search Console.
